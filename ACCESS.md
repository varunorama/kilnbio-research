# Access to the KilnBio research release

Request permission at **varun.dwaraka@gmail.com**. The [license](LICENSE) governs original KilnBio material. Approved users may analyze their own data, adapt the software internally for the approved study and publish their findings. No publication veto or coauthorship is required.

| Proposed activity | Permission |
|---|---|
| Academic lab analyzes a grant-funded cohort and publishes figures | Within an approved noncommercial project |
| Approved research team tests a change on its institutional compute cluster | Within that project, with access limited to the approved users |
| A new lab member, separate collaboration or different study wants to use the package | Request an amendment or new approval first |
| A university core is paid to run samples for a client; a company evaluates a product; a group trains a model for commercial deployment | Separate commercial agreement required |
| A company funds the study or has commercialization rights | Disclose sponsor and terms for explicit review before use |
| Posting the ZIP publicly, forwarding it outside the approved group, or distributing a modified fork | Outside the research license |

**Request template.** Name the investigator, institution, proposed users, release tag, study purpose, expected duration, funding and any client/sponsor rights. State whether the work involves paid services, commercial model development or hosting. Do not include participant information or data.

**Approval template for the owner.** “I approve [names] at [institution] to use KilnBio [tag] under the attached KilnBio Noncommercial Research License 1.0 for [study] until [date, or completion]. No onward distribution is authorized. Please reply accepting these terms before starting the study.” Have an authorized representative accept if an institution is the licensee. Amendments and commercial agreements should be recorded separately.

**Getting a release.** Anyone can view the code and download the tagged archives from the [research releases](https://github.com/varunorama/kilnbio-research/releases). Download the named ZIP and its matching SHA-256 file. Compare the checksum before extraction. The [license](LICENSE) requires project-specific written approval before running or adapting KilnBio. Commercial and industry-sponsored uses require separate permission. The public download does not grant those rights.
