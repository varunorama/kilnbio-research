# Research use and interpretation

**KilnBio is intended for research use only. It has not been validated for clinical diagnosis, patient screening, treatment selection or patient management. It is not intended for diagnostic procedures.**

Researchers can use the supported workflows to process molecular data, estimate associations, compare analysis choices and prioritize candidates for further experiments. Public-data examples assess those research tasks. A successful software test, reproduction of a published association or held-out research comparison does not establish clinical validity or clinical utility.

In particular:

- Variant calls and ClinVar annotations are research evidence. ClinVar review status, conflicting assertions and germline versus somatic classifications must remain visible. An annotation does not diagnose a participant.
- The clonal hematopoiesis interface screens candidate variants or imports provider classifications. It does not establish a clinical CHIP diagnosis, and low-variant-allele-fraction sensitivity has not been established by the software checks.
- Aging scores and molecular biomarker outputs are model-specific research measurements. Their availability does not validate their use for individual health assessment.
- AlphaMissense and AlphaGenome outputs are predictions. AlphaFold Database supplies structural context. These are not independent measured assays, experimental confirmation or clinical classifications assigned by KilnBio.
- Candidate targets nominated by multi-omic comparisons require appropriate independent and experimental follow-up. Method agreement does not establish causation or therapeutic benefit.

A clinical laboratory may conduct research, but clinical use of a workflow requires its own applicable validation, quality systems and regulatory assessment. A research-use statement does not itself determine regulatory status. In the United States, CLIA requirements concern testing human specimens for health assessment or diagnosis, prevention or treatment of disease; the research exception is conditional on how participant-specific results are used and reported. See [CMS CLIA regulations and compliance](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-safety-standards/clinical-laboratory-improvement-amendments-clia/clia-regulations-compliance) and the [CDC overview](https://www.cdc.gov/clia/php/about/). ClinVar separately explains its [data-use and diagnostic limitations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/docs/maintenance_use/).
