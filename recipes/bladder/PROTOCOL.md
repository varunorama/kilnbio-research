# Frozen transport test: TCGA bladder cancer

Fixed 2026-11-02 before calculating or viewing ranks in this cohort. This is a new-cohort reuse of the lung study's three-arm ranking decision, not external validation of lung genes. No expected results are shipped with the researcher instructions.

* **Contrast:** Primary bladder tumor samples (TCGA sample type 01) in the mutation-profiled roster. Compare cases with a reported FGFR3 protein-altering somatic SNV/indel vs those with no such call, excluding FGFR3 itself as a candidate. Count missense, nonsense, nonstop, splice-site, translation-start, frameshift and in-frame insertion/deletion calls. Do not label negatives fusion-free or genetically wild type. One clinical specimen per patient; require an exact deposited specimen ID for both assays. Do not infer aliquot identity.
* **Population:** A = eligible primary RNA-profiled patients; B = RNA in the subset with RPPA; P = RPPA in exactly B; C = joint B and P. Report both genotype groups in the full, matched and excluded populations. Stop if pairing is ambiguous, either group has fewer than three patients per fitted arm, no RNA-only population remains or fewer than 15 candidates qualify.
* **Shared universe:** Use unique RNA symbols and one eligible RPPA row per gene; exclude ambiguous/multi-gene symbols, modified/cleaved or mutation-specific antibodies identifiable from the label and the grouping gene. Record all mappings. Require at least 80% finite observations in each group and nonzero variance for a feature in each fitted matrix (A, B and P); no imputation. Restrict all arms to the same features. Use log2(1 + nonnegative RSEM abundance); use deposited continuous RPPA values without logging them again.
* **Ranking:** Intercept and binary group, ordinary limma empirical Bayes moderated t for the positive-minus-negative contrast, without outcome-informed covariate selection. Within each arm score = sign(t) × average rank of |t| / (number of eligible genes + 1); C = arithmetic mean of the two matched signed scores. Sort by decreasing absolute score, breaking ties by gene in byte order. Fix 15 nominations per arm. Do not optimize the budget or change protein weights on these data.
* **Endpoint:** For A→B (restrict patients) and B→C (add protein), report retained, entered and exited genes and any retained sign reversals. Report A→C as context. This isolates the two changes to the *analysis*, not their biological causes. The selected common panel restricts inference even in A. No accuracy, treatment benefit, independent biological replication or improvement in target quality can be inferred from this cohort alone.
* **Usability test:** An unfamiliar bioinformatician uses only the versioned archive, its README, public inputs and listed dependencies. Return the report, named changes, stage times, manual edits and help needed. Judge numerical reproduction separately from unassisted completion. An author-side rehearsal cannot satisfy the latter.
