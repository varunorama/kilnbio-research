KilnBio 0.2.0-research.1 research release (Linux/WSL2)

PREPROCESSING ADAPTERS (pinned nf-core; explicitly recorded in provenance)
  kilnbio {wgbs|rrbs|atacseq|chipseq|rnaseq|scrnaseq} samples.csv params.json RUN_DIR
          [--dry-run] [--resume] [--profile docker|apptainer|singularity]
          [--cpus N] [--memory-gb N]

Use explicit local references, assay chemistry and biological sample metadata.
Outputs are immutable per run. Failed runs remain for inspection; retry into a
new directory. Nothing automatically deletes raw data. See docs/USER_GUIDE.md.
KILNBIO_PYTHON selects an analysis environment; KILNBIO_RSCRIPT and
KILNBIO_R_LIBRARY select R and its private library. Containers are used by the
preprocessing adapters. The featurecounts command also supports pinned Docker.
